About us

This Interior was founded in 2020 by Eliza Webber, an interior designer who wanted to turn ordinary rooms into places people want to stay in. Design shapes how a space feels and how people behave inside it, and the studio was built around that one idea.

The early work was residential: redesigning homes and shaping living spaces around how each client actually lives. Attention to detail, listening before drawing, and a willingness to work with a room's existing character are what the studio became known for.

As the practice grew it took on larger and more complex work — commercial spaces, offices and hospitality interiors — with a small team of designers and project managers working alongside Eliza.

The studio still works the way it started: one room at a time, finished properly, and photographed only once it is done.
